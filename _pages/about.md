I am Marco Rossi, a PhD candidate at the University of Toronto Climate Modeling Group. My research focuses on climate science and machine learning for Earth systems. I graduated from the University of Bologna in July 2022, and I am currently pursuing my Ph.D. in Earth Sciences at the University of Toronto.

Research Interests
======
* Deep Learning for Weather Forecasting
* Climate Model Emulation
* Extreme Event Attribution

Academic Background
======
* Ph.D. in Earth Sciences, University of Toronto (2022 - Present)
* Laurea, University of Bologna (2017 - 2022)

Research Experience
======

* Research Intern, ECMWF (May 2024 - October 2024)
